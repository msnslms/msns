🚔 About the Traffic Unit

Our Student Traffic Warden Unit is dedicated to maintaining a safe environment around the school premises. With the professional support of the Sri Lanka Police, these student leaders develop essential life skills while performing a crucial service. From guiding fellow students across the road to managing school-hour traffic, our wardens ensure that every student reaches home safely. It is a platform where discipline meets duty, empowering our students to become responsible citizens of tomorrow.
