About the Media Unit

Methsen Swara is the premier morning broadcast program organized and produced by the School Media Unit. This program serves as a vibrant start to the school day, fostering an environment of information, inspiration, and creativity.


The program is brought to life by the talented young voices of our school. Students who possess exceptional skills in announcing, broadcasting, and public speaking are given the platform to showcase their abilities, ensuring a high standard of delivery and engagement. Our unit works tirelessly to bridge the gap between information and the student body, building a confident generation ready to face the world of communication.

About Her


Since 2021, Mrs. Tharanga Kumuduni Hettiarachchi has been a driving force behind the Media Studies department of our college, rendering a service that is both visionary and transformative. Her unwavering commitment has been the cornerstone in establishing the "Methsen Swara" Media Unit, which stands today as a beacon of excellence and creativity.


Under her expert guidance, the Media Unit has evolved into a powerhouse for nurturing young talent. By identifying and honing theiverse skills of students, "Methsen Swara" produces a generation of confident, versatile, and skilled communicators ready to serve the nation. Whether in broadcasting, technical production, or creative journalism, these students are being groomed to lead the future of the media landscape.


This remarkable journey is a testament to Mrs. Hettiarachchi's passion for education and her dedication to empowering the next generation of media professionals.
